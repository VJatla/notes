* Chaddi annam
** Ingredients
- 1.5 cup cooked rice
- 1 cup warm milk
- 1 cup warm water
- 2 Tbsp Butter milk (or curd)
- Salt
  
** Method
- Pour warm milk over rice till the rice is totally immersed. Add a few drops of Buttermilk.
- Add pieces of onions + green chilli (optional).
