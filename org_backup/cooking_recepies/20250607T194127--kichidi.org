* Kichidi

** Vel pongal

Tamilian style.

1. *Dry roast*

   - 1 Cup rice + 1 Cup moong dal --- /Dry roast on low flame until they give aroma./
   
2. *Cook*

   - Step 1 + 4 cups water --- /Cook in instapot under rice setting./

3. *Temper*

   - Ingredients: cashews, curry leaves, crushed black pepper, cumin,
     mustard seeds, split green chilli, grated ginger.

   - tempering ingredients --- /Fry in ghee/

4. *Mix*

   - Step 3+ tempering ingredients --- /Mix, add hot water as needed./
