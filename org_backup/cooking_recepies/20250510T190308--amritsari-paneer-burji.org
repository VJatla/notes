* Amritsari Paneer Bhurji

** Making masala
1. Roast 2 tbsp besan on low flame until fragrant.
2. After besan cools add 3/4th cup curd, 1tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp
   grab, 1 tsp chaat masala, 1tsp pav bhaji masala.
** Tomato onion base
1. 1tbsp oil + 1tbsp butters --> /Let it heat/
2. 1tsp cumin seeds + 1tbsp kasuri meethi crushed + 5 cloves of garlic (chopped) --> /saute/
3. Add 1 jumbo chopped onions --> /fry till color change/
4. 1/2 tsp turmeric + 1tsp chilli powder --> /Saute on low flame/
5. Add 1 chopped tomato --> /Let it cook till mushy/
** Mix
1. Add masala to tomato onion base --> /Cook till oil seperates./
2. Add 3/4th cup of hot milk --> /Mix well/
3. Add 200g paneer + little bit chopped onion + little bit chopped tomato + little bit bell pepper + cilantro --> /Add after turning off the heat./
