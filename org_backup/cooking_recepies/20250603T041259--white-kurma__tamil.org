* White kurma

I have seen this kurma eaten with parotta during evenings at
Tamilnadu. I am taking this recepie from [[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HFZbyJs41VI][YouTube Vismai food]].

** Masa paste

*** Ingredients
- 3/4th cup fresh coconut
- 3 Spicy Green chillies
- 1 Tbsp Saunf
- 1/2 inch Ginger
- 6-7 Garlic pods
- 5 Cloves
- 1 Inch Cinnamon
- 3 Green Cardamom
- 1/4 cup Cashew (30 min. soaked)
- Roasted Chana Dal (Putnalu)

  
*** Process
Grind to fine paste. Instead of water it will be better to add milk
(vegan is also fine).

** Veggies

- Green peas (Pachhi batani)
- French beans
- One potato peeled and cut into small cubes
- One Carrot cut into small cubes
- 1/4th Cauliflower cut into small florets
  
** Cooking

1. Pressure cooker pot --> Hot
2. 1 Tbsp oil + 1 Tbsp Ghee + Stone flower + 1 Bay leaf --> Fry for 2 minutes
3. Step 2 + Veggies --> Fry for 5 minutes
4. Step 3 + Masala paste + 400 ml water --> 4 minutes on Instant pot
   - Alternate: /2 whistles on medium flame and 1 whistle on hight flame on stove/
