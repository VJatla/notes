* Nomenclature

- 1T = 1 Table spoon
- 1t = 1 tea spoon
- 1C = 1 cup
  
* Wamu annam

1. urud dal [1T], Chana dal [1T], zeera [1/2T], musturd seeds [1/2T] -- /Fry until chanadal color changes./
2. Step 1 + Wamu or Ajwain [1/2T]
3. Step 2 + split green chillies [2] + red chilies [2] + curry leaves [1 stem] + onion [1/4] -- /Cook until onion becomes translucent./
4. Step 3 + turmeric [1t] + salt + cooked rice [2 C], old is better  -- /Mix/
