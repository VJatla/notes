* Wamu annam

1. 1 Tbsp urud dal, 1 Tbsp Chana dal, 1/2 Tbsp zeera, 1/2 Tbsp musturd seeds -- /Fry until chanadal color changes./
2. Step 1 + 1/2 Tbsp Wamu or Ajwain
3. Step 2 + 2 split green chillies + 2 red chilies + 1 stem curry leaves + small amout of onions -- /Cook until onion becomes translucent./
4. Step 3 + 1 tsp turmeric + salt + cooked rice (old rice is better) -- /Mix/
