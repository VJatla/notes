* Chicken pakodi (street style)

** Masala

Add all these and mix well

#+begin_src bash
	500g chicken           + salt to taste + 1/4 tsp turmeric power +
	1/2 slcie lemon juice  + 2 tsp red chilli powder + 1/2 tsp  cumin power +
	1 tsp corriander powder + 1/2 tsp pepper power + 2 tsp garam masala +
	2 tsp ginger garlic paste 
#+end_src

** Flour coating

Mix these in, add water for consistency. It should be sticking to the chicken peaces.

#+begin_src bash
  1 Tbsp corn flour + 1 Tbsp rice flour + 2 Tbsp Gram flour 
#+end_src

** Garnishes

Mix in chopped green chili, chopped mint leaves, chopped curry leaves, chopped corriander leaves + 1 Tbsp oil

** Rest

1 hour outside, or 30 minutes in fridge.


** Deep fry (or shallow fry)

Cook in medium-high heat.
