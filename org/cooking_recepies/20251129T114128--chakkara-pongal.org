* Chakkara pongal

- 1/4th cup of moong dal (tasted until fragrant)
- 3/4th cup rice
- 2 cups water
- 1.5 cups Jaggery (metlt with .5 cup water)
- Dry fruits as required (Cashew and raisins recommended)
